FAQ

Questions people actually ask

Is there a free trial?

Yes — every feature is unlocked for 7 days, no credit card required. After that, a one-time license removes the trial banner for good.

Does Dense need an internet connection?

No. Compression runs entirely on your Mac. The only time Dense talks to the network is to activate your license after purchase.

What file formats can Dense compress?

Video: MOV, and common containers Dense re-encodes to H.264/HEVC MOV. Images: JPEG, PNG, HEIC, TIFF, WebP, BMP. Plus GIF optimization, video-to-GIF, MP3 audio extraction, and PDF compression.

Can Dense compress password-protected PDFs?

Not yet — password-protected PDFs are currently unsupported and Dense will tell you clearly rather than fail silently.

What's the refund policy?

Refunds are available within a reasonable window of purchase if Dense doesn't work for you — details finalized at launch.

Does it work on Apple Silicon and Intel Macs?

Yes. Dense ships as a universal binary and runs natively on both, requiring macOS 13 or later.

macOS 13+ · Apple Silicon & Intel. Dense uses FFmpeg under the GPL, run as a separate process — build sources: evermeet.cx (x86_64) and osxexperts.net (arm64).
