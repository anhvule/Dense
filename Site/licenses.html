FFmpeg

Dense bundles FFmpeg 7.1 (https://ffmpeg.org), licensed under the GNU General Public License, version 2 or later. Dense invokes the bundled ffmpeg and ffprobe binaries as separate processes and does not link against FFmpeg's libraries.

Corresponding source

  • Upstream FFmpeg 7.1 release source: https://ffmpeg.org/releases/ffmpeg-7.1.tar.xz
  • The bundled arm64 build was obtained from osxexperts.net.
  • The bundled x86_64 build was obtained from evermeet.cx.

This page serves as Dense's written offer for the corresponding source of the exact FFmpeg builds it ships. If you need a copy of the source and cannot obtain it from the links above, contact us and we will provide it.
