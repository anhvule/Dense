Video to GIF

Convert a video clip straight into a GIF in one drop — no separate converter app needed.

How it works

Turn on GIF mode (Convert to GIF) in the advanced panel before dropping a video, or use the destination flow if it's exposed for your file. Dense runs a two-pass palette generation and application (palettegen/paletteuse under the hood) so the resulting GIF looks noticeably cleaner than a naive single-pass conversion.

Advanced panel options

  • fps — a stepper from 5 to 30, default 12. Lower values shrink file size further at the cost of smoothness.
  • width — a stepper from 240 to 960 in steps of 80, default 480.
Unlike re-compressing an existing GIF, video-to-GIF conversion always applies your chosen fps — it's generating new frames from the source video, not just re-palettizing what's already there.

Output location, suffix, and cleanup

The converted GIF follows the same shared options: Output (Next to original or a Custom folder), Suffix (default -compressed), and Move to Trash after success for the original video, if you want it gone.

macOS 13+ · Apple Silicon & Intel. Dense uses FFmpeg under the GPL, run as a separate process — build sources: evermeet.cx (x86_64) and osxexperts.net (arm64).
