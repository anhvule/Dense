License FAQ

Good to know

Is this really a one-time purchase?

Yes. $29 ($19 for early customers) once. No subscription, no renewal, no expiring license.

Do future major versions cost extra?

All updates within the 1.x line are free. A future paid upgrade for a major new version (2.0) hasn't been decided — nothing is being planned to break what you already own.

How many Macs can I use it on?

The license is currently planned to cover two Macs per purchase. This will be confirmed before checkout goes live.

What happens when the trial ends?

Dense stops running new compressions until you enter a license key. Anything you already compressed stays exactly as it is.

What if I want a refund?

Refunds are available within a reasonable window of purchase if Dense isn't a fit — full terms will be posted at launch.

Does the license work offline?

Once activated, yes — Dense checks in only occasionally, and compression itself never requires a connection.
