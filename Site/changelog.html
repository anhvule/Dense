What's new in Dense

Every release, in plain language.

v1.0.0 — 2026-07-10

First public release.

  • Video compression to H.264 or HEVC MOV, hardware-accelerated with VideoToolbox
  • Target-size mode — compress to a specific MB ceiling
  • Resolution caps (4K/1080p/720p) and audio removal
  • Destination-dock presets: Discord, Email, YouTube, Web/Social, and a fully custom preset
  • Image compression for JPEG, PNG, HEIC, TIFF, WebP, and BMP with EXIF preserved
  • GIF optimization for existing GIFs, and one-drop video-to-GIF conversion
  • Extract MP3 audio from any video, falling back to AAC automatically
  • PDF compression on Apple's native Quartz engine — no Ghostscript install, text stays selectable
  • Batch queue with per-file isolation and real cancellation
  • Move-originals-to-Trash option, custom output folder and filename suffix
  • Sparkle auto-updates
  • 7-day full-feature trial; one-time license, no subscription
