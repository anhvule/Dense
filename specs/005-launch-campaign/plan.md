# Implementation Plan: 1-Month Launch Campaign

**Branch**: `005-launch-campaign` | **Spec**: [spec.md](spec.md) | **Source**: `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-07-09-launch-campaign-playbook.md`

## Summary
Operational, not code. Strategy: the product demo is the marketing; before/after clips are natively shareable; convert attention to an owned email waitlist; concentrate into one coordinated launch day.

## Technical Context
**Channels**: X, TikTok/Reels/Shorts, Reddit (r/VideoEditing, r/NewTubers, r/Twitch, r/macapps), Product Hunt, Show HN, one newsletter sponsorship
**Tools**: Buttondown (free), Cloudflare Registrar/Pages, Lemon Squeezy (`LAUNCH35`)
**Constraints**: $500 budget, zero starting audience

## Constitution Check
VI (truthful copy) binds hardest on ads; copy dispatches carry a product-truth block and a reviewer truthfulness sweep. VII: no iOS/web funnel.

## Timeline
| Week | Theme | Exit criteria |
|---|---|---|
| 1 (d1–7) | Foundation | Name+domain, waitlist live, 7 days posts, first clip |
| 2 (d8–14) | Audience | Waitlist ≥100, a clip >5k views, Reddit/Discord standing |
| 3 (d15–21) | Beta & proof | 1.0.0 DMG, checkout, ≥3 testimonials, PH assets, newsletter booked; waitlist ≥300 |
| 4 (d22–30) | Launch | PH Tue, HN Wed, Reddit/newsletter/X ads, momentum, retro |

## Post-launch gates (from master harness, macOS only)
v1.1 backlog: savings ledger ("you've saved 47 GB"), per-file remove, output-path collision uniquing, LicenseClient mocked tests. Never watermark outputs.

## Budget
Domain+tools $50 · Newsletter $300 · X ads test $100 → $450 / $500.
